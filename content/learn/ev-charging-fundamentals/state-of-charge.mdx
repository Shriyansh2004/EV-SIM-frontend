## What is SoC?

**State of Charge (SoC)** represents how full an EV battery is, expressed as a percentage from 0% (empty) to 100% (full). It's the EV equivalent of a fuel gauge.

## How SoC is determined

In real vehicles, SoC is estimated by the Battery Management System (BMS) using:

- **Voltage measurement** — battery voltage correlates with charge level
- **Coulomb counting** — tracking current in/out over time
- **Temperature compensation** — adjusting for thermal effects

## SoC during charging

As energy flows into the battery, SoC increases. The relationship isn't perfectly linear — charging slows near 80–100% (the "taper") to protect battery health. EV-SIM uses a simplified linear model for simulation.

| SoC range | Charging behavior |
| --- | --- |
| 0–80% | Full power delivery |
| 80–95% | Reduced power (taper begins) |
| 95–100% | Trickle charge |

## SoC in OCPP

OCPP doesn't define a standard SoC field in core messages. However, `MeterValues` can include custom measurands, and `TransactionEvent` payloads may carry EV-specific data. EV-SIM tracks SoC internally and displays it in the live charts.

<Callout variant="tip" title="EV-SIM tip">
Watch the SoC chart on the charger detail page during an active session — it updates in real time as MeterValues are reported.
</Callout>

## Why SoC matters for operators

- **Session planning** — estimate remaining charge time
- **Load management** — prioritize sessions nearing completion
- **Grid integration** — coordinate charging with renewable availability
- **User experience** — display progress in mobile apps

## Two roles

OCPP defines exactly two roles in the charging architecture:

| Role | Also known as | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Charge Point** | Charging Station, EVSE | At the charging site |
| **Central System** | CSMS, Central Management System | Cloud / data center |

## Charge Point responsibilities

The charge point is the **edge device** at the physical location:

- Manages connectors and power delivery
- Sends boot, heartbeat, and status messages
- Reports meter values and transaction events
- Executes remote commands from the CSMS
- Handles local authorization (RFID, etc.)

In EV-SIM, each virtual charger you create acts as a charge point.

## Central System responsibilities

The CSMS is the **control plane**:

- Accepts and manages charge point connections
- Authorizes charging sessions
- Issues remote start/stop commands
- Stores session and metering data
- Provides APIs for operator tools
- Manages configuration and firmware

## Asymmetric capabilities

Not every OCPP action can be initiated by both sides:

| Initiated by CP | Initiated by CSMS |
| --- | --- |
| BootNotification | RequestStartTransaction |
| Heartbeat | RequestStopTransaction |
| StatusNotification | Reset |
| TransactionEvent | ChangeAvailability |
| MeterValues | GetVariables / SetVariables |
| NotifyReport | TriggerMessage |

<Callout variant="info" title="Think of it this way">
The Charge Point is the "client" at the edge — it reports what happens. The CSMS is the "server" in the cloud — it decides what should happen.
</Callout>

## Multiple charge points

A single CSMS manages many charge points simultaneously. Each maintains its own WebSocket connection identified by its charge point ID.

## Starting a session

### Remote start

From the charger detail page, click **Remote Start**. This sends a `RequestStartTransaction` from the CSMS to the charge point, which responds with `TransactionEvent (Started)`.

### What happens next

1. Connector status changes to `Occupied`
2. Energy delivery begins at the configured max power
3. `MeterValues` are reported at regular intervals
4. SoC increases on the live chart

## Stopping a session

Click **Remote Stop** to end the session. The charge point sends `TransactionEvent (Ended)` with the final meter reading.

## Session data

During and after a session, you can view:

- **Energy delivered** (kWh)
- **Duration**
- **Average power** (kW)
- **SoC change** (start → end)
- **Transaction ID** (from OCPP)

## Sessions page

The **Sessions** page (`/sessions`) lists all historical sessions across your fleet with filtering and sorting.

## Live charts

During an active charging session, the charger detail page displays real-time charts updated as `MeterValues` arrive from the charge point.

## Chart types

### Power (kW)

Shows instantaneous power delivery. Should stabilize near the charger's max power rating during active charging, then drop during the taper phase near full SoC.

### Energy (kWh)

Cumulative energy delivered since session start. Increases monotonically — it never decreases during a session.

### State of Charge (%)

Battery fill level of the connected EV. Starts at the session's initial SoC and increases as energy is delivered.

## Reading the charts

| Pattern | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Flat power line at max kW | Normal charging at full rate |
| Declining power, rising SoC | Taper phase (80%+) |
| Power drops to zero | Session ended or paused |
| Gaps in data | Missed MeterValues intervals |

<Callout variant="info" title="Update frequency">

## Historical data

After a session ends, the session detail page retains the chart data for review. Use the **Sessions** page to browse historical sessions and their energy profiles.
